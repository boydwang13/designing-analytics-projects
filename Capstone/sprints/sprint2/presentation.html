WS2 · Network Metrics — 中心性 + Boundary Spanning (Martin #1)

依据与原理 Rationale

In-degree → Hub 识别（Cross & Parker central connector）；Betweenness → Broker 识别（Granovetter bridge）；Cross_Team_Tie_Count → Boundary Spanner 操作化（每人跨越了几个 Team）。仅使用你批准的两篇参考文献，不引入 Burt structural holes。

In-degree flags hubs (Cross & Parker central connector); betweenness flags brokers (Granovetter bridge); Cross_Team_Tie_Count operationalizes Cross & Parker's boundary spanner (how many distinct teams each employee reaches). Burt's structural-hole measures were out of scope by decision.

产出 Deliverables
  • src/metrics.py — 300 节点全量指标（degree / strength / betweenness / Cross_Team_Tie_Count / Weak_Tie_Outbound_Count / Weak_Cross_Team_Tie_Count）
  • outputs/sprint2_power_user_concentration.md — Top-5 Hub inbound share, Herfindahl index
  • outputs/sprint2_brokers_shortlist.md — Top-10 brokers（betweenness + Cross_Team_Tie_Count tie-break）

WS3 · Isolation Score v1.0

依据与原理 Rationale

4 组件等权（w=0.25 each）：OutboundScarcity + WeakWeightShare + TargetConcentration + WeakBridgeDeficit。每项归一化到 [0, 1]；零出向节点被定义为 1.0（最孤立）。公式与归一化方法同步写入 codebook。

Four equally weighted (0.25 each) sub-components: OutboundScarcity + WeakWeightShare + TargetConcentration + WeakBridgeDeficit. Each normalized to [0, 1]; zero-outbound nodes defined as 1.0 (maximally isolated). Formula and normalization documented in the codebook.

产出 Deliverables
  • src/isolation_score.py — 可独立运行的 4-component scorer
  • Isolation_Score 列加入 sprint2_nodes_with_metrics.csv

四、团队间密度矩阵 Team-to-Team Directed Block Density (Cross & Parker, 2004)

读图说明：行 = 求助发起 Team，列 = 被求助 Team。对角线（深蓝框）= 团队内部求助密度；非对角线 = 跨团队求助密度。颜色越深密度越高。

How to read: rows = source team (who asks), columns = target team (who is asked). Diagonal cells (dark-blue outline) = intra-team density; off-diagonal = cross-team. Deeper color = higher density.

关键发现 · Key findings in current snapshot

① 所有 6 个 team 的 intra/off-diagonal 比率都 < 1（Data Engineering 最低 0.37，Client Services 最高 0.97）——没有 team 呈现 silo 特征。这是对 Martin "是否有整个团队变成孤岛" 的硬数据答复：在当前合成数据中未出现。

② Analytics & BI 是 净助人方（inbound 0.086 vs outbound 0.066）。

③ Data Engineering 是 最向外依赖的团队（内部 0.005 vs 对 Analytics & BI 的 0.017，3.4× 不对称）。

① All six teams' intra/off-diagonal ratios are < 1 (lowest 0.37 Data Engineering, highest 0.97 Client Services) — no team displays a classical silo pattern in synthetic data, directly answering Martin's question.

② Analytics & BI is a net help provider (inbound 0.086 > outbound 0.066).

③ Data Engineering is the most outward-dependent team (its own internal density 0.005 is only 1/3.4 of its density toward Analytics & BI at 0.017).
